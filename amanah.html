Amanah

Ethical Technology Ecosystem

A future technology ecosystem designed around privacy, responsibility and ethical principles.

What we are building Follow our progress

Why Amanah

Technology you can trust.

Amanah means trust. In technology, trust has to be part of the design from the first day, not added afterwards.

KB Group is building Amanah to show that useful technology can respect the people who use it.

Principles

Three things we will not trade away.

Privacy first

Collect only what is needed, explain why, and keep control with the person who owns the data.

Responsibility

Take accountability for how our products are used and for what they affect.

Ethical principles

Check decisions about design, data and growth against clear ethical principles.

What we are building

An ecosystem, not a single app.

Amanah is planned as a set of connected products and services that share the same principles and the same standard of care.

Details of each product will be shared here as they are ready.

Shared foundations

One approach to privacy, one approach to accountability and one set of ethical rules across everything Amanah makes.
