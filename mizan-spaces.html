About Mizan Spaces

Spaces that are in balance.

Mizan means balance. Every project we take on looks for the right balance between how a space works, how well it is built and how it looks and feels.

Mizan Spaces is a business of KB Group, and it works to the same values: trust, fairness and care for the people who use what we build.

What guides our work

Function, quality and aesthetics.

Functionality

Layouts that suit how people live and work, planned before anything is built.

Quality

Sound materials, careful workmanship and checks at each stage of the build.

Aesthetics

Clean lines, natural materials and light, so a space feels good to be in.
